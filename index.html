Common questions

Before you
commit.

Are the parcels NA-certified?
+
Five of the seven holdings are NA-complete (Sodhi-712, Pipli-338, Valinda-277, Bhangadh-133, and the Classic Smart Homes layout). Two are mid-process (Sangasar-535 and Gorasu-1021); we share the collector's provisional approval letters on request. NA conversion in Dholera SIR is a procedural step inside DSIRDA, not a hurdle.
How does payment & registration work?
+
Standard sub-registrar registration in Ahmedabad district. We accept staged payment for parcels above ₹1 crore — typically 20% on agreement, 70% on registration, 10% on possession. Stamp duty and registration are buyer's account, currently 4.9% combined in Gujarat. We provide the title chain, 7/12 record, NA order, and DSIRDA verification before the agreement.
What is the resale liquidity in Dholera?
+
Resale liquidity is asymmetric across TP zones. TP-1 (residential, near airport) and TP-4B (industrial, near ABCD Building) move within weeks of listing. Outer TP-3C and TP-5C move on quarters, not weeks. We share zone-level transaction velocity from our own ledger when relevant to your selection.
Who is DSIRDA, and what does that mean for my title?
+
The Dholera Special Investment Regional Development Authority is a body of the Government of Gujarat. It notified the master plan, runs the Town Planning schemes that produced the 27 TP zones, and issues the Final Plot (FP) numbers under which titles are registered. Holdings inside a notified TP carry a stronger legal floor than agricultural land outside the SIR — they are surveyed, fenced, and titled under direct government oversight.
Can NRIs buy?
+
Yes, for residential and mixed-use parcels under the FEMA general permission. Industrial parcels require RBI approval depending on the entity structure; for OCI/PIO holders, the residential route is straightforward. We coordinate documentation with the buyer's CA and a Gujarat-registered conveyancing lawyer.
What is the minimum allocation size?
+
Inside Classic Smart Homes, plots start at 49 sq.yd (compact, end-user) — entry tickets begin around ₹15–20 lakh. For the standalone parcels (Sodhi, Sangasar, Bhangadh, etc.), the minimum sale unit is 100 sq.yd per Gujarat regulation, though most allocators take 1,000+ sq.yd given the price-per-sq.yd. Family-office mandates are typically structured between ₹1 cr and ₹5 cr across one or two TPs.
